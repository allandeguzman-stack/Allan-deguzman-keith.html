LOOKING BACK, MOVING FORWARD

My reflection.

“

Before taking this course, I thought literature was just old books written long ago. I imagined it as something distant, disconnected from my life—from my phone, my social media, and the fast-paced world I live in. But studying 21st-century literature has changed that view. I have learned that literature is alive, and it evolves just as we do.

What struck me most is how accessible and relatable modern literature can be. Stories can be short, shared online, and written by ordinary people—not just famous authors. I realized that my own experiences, my family's stories, and even the way I speak can be valid material for literature.

This course also taught me to value both our heritage and our future. Reading works from different regions of the Philippines showed me the richness of our diversity, while digital literature opened my eyes to new ways of telling stories. We do not have to choose between tradition and progress; we can carry our roots with us as we move forward.

Moving forward, I want to read more widely and write more freely. I want to preserve the stories of my family and community and share them through whatever means I can. Literature is not just a school requirement—it is one way we keep our memories alive, connect with others, and understand who we are.
